Design system

Ledger

A pattern, not a component: a statement table where one entry can produce another that belongs to it — the fee a transfer charged, the reversal of a charge. It is assembled from DataTable pieces and needs no ledger-specific API.

02Anatomy

Each part maps onto an existing DataTable piece. The pattern is in how they combine.

PartPieceRule
Day dividerDTGroupDivider metaDay on the left; on the right, muted and tabular, the count and the day's net of settled entries.
Parent rowDTParentRow kind="subrows" rowAction="open"Columns Description · Time · Method · Status · Amount · Balance. A click opens the detail.
ChevronDTSubrowToggle / spacerOnly on a row with a fee. A row without one keeps the 20px with spacer, so names stay aligned.
Direction tile.ic-tileMoney in success · internal transfer info · money out neutral. Tone carries direction, never status.
NameDTRowButtonThe keyboard handle for opening — a real button that looks like the text.
Amount.dt-numMono. In: .delta-pos. Out: default. Failed or returned: the row is .is-muted and the figure is struck. With a fee, a muted second line: "total −18,450.00 with fee".
Balance.dt-num.dt-balanceThe balance after the entry, regular and muted. Only settled entries have one; the rest show "—".
FeeDTSubrowLead · DTSubrowMeta · DTSubrowCellIts own entry: receipt icon, name, "Fee" tag, time, "Charged with the transfer", amount and balance.

05Rules

WhenDo
Use itStatements and ledgers where an entry generates another that belongs to it. A flat list of payments is a plain table.
Running balanceThe fee is always newer than its transfer at the same instant, so the transfer's balance comes first and the fee's balance is lower.
Row clickrowAction="open" when the row has a detail drawer; "toggle" (the default) when the group is the only thing to reveal.
AccessibilityNative table, no treegrid. The fee carries an sr-only "Item of <parent>:" prefix; the chevron has aria-expanded and aria-controls pointing at the fee row; the name is a button. Motion respects prefers-reduced-motion.
