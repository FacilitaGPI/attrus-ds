Design system
Components · Data · new in v1.5.0

Charts

Four chart components built on the data-viz tokens and the track and team colours. Each one draws its colours from --seg; on the dark theme every part carries a thin white edge, because the Enterprise track colour (#002142) disappears against the surface.

DistributionBar

A total split into parts, as one 8px bar with 2px gaps. The legend underneath is a grid (key, name, value, %) so values and percentages line up between rows. A part never draws narrower than 3px, and a non-zero part under 1% reads "<1%". A key of a track renders the TrackBadge.

StageDistribution

The compact funnel: one part per stage, as wide as its count, with the stage key and count underneath. Shades of the track colour run from 100% to 32%, mixed with the surface rather than made transparent, so the tone holds on any background. A column is at least 36px so the label fits.

BarChart

Columns per period, series stacked (or side by side with stacked={false}). The selected period stays at full opacity and the rest drops to 35%. valueLabel puts the last column's total above it, with 15% headroom reserved in the scale. Month axis in mono. No gridlines: the label and the legend carry the reading. A screen-reader-only table repeats every number.

Sparkline

A mini chart under a KPI. bars: seven 6px bars, 2px apart, up to 18px, only the last in the tone. line: a 1.5px line with a dot on the last value, in the same 54×18 box. Decorative by default (the KPI beside it carries the number); pass label to expose it.
